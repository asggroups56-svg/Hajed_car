You can request the deletion of your Bin Wazir Cars account and associated personal data using the instructions on this page.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to erp@delta-asg.com .
  2. Use the subject: Account Deletion Request - Bin Wazir Cars.
  3. Include the email address associated with your account and any information reasonably needed to identify your account.
  4. We may contact you to verify your identity before processing your request.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

Once your request is verified and processed, we will delete your account and associated personal data that is not required to be retained.

Certain records, such as transaction or financial records, may be retained where required by applicable law. Any retained data will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements and our privacy policy.

How Long Does Deletion Take?

We will review your request, verify account ownership where necessary, and process it as soon as reasonably practicable, subject to applicable legal requirements.
